FAQ

Questions, answered

Is the Free plan really free?

Yes — $0, no credit card, no trial clock. To pay for the servers, free users see occasional sponsored messages. Upgrade to Ultimate ($23/mo) or Teams ($19/user/mo) to remove them and unlock bigger limits, custom branding and more.

What are the Free plan limits?

10 transfers per month, up to 3 GB of total transfer volume per month, transfers up to 10 MB each, and expiry of at most 3 days. Once a transfer is created the view limit and expiry you chose still apply.

What does "zero-knowledge" mean here?

Your content is encrypted with a key generated inside your browser. That key only travels inside the link's fragment — the part after # — which browsers never send to our servers. We store ciphertext only, and delete it when your transfer expires or its views run out.

Can I remove the ads?

Yes. Any Ultimate or Teams plan is 100% ad-free, and your recipients see your own branding instead of sponsors.

Can I pay per user for a team?

Teams is $19 per user per month with a minimum of 2 users, and supports up to 50 members with centralized billing and team workspaces. Sign up free, then create your team from the dashboard.
